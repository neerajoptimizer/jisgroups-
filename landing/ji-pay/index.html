How it works

FAQ

Questions operators ask

What is a Bank MQR payment solution?

A bank-based merchant QR payment rail that lets players pay with UPI apps. JI PAY supports both UPI Intent (the UPI app opens directly) and Quasi-Intent flows.

How fast are deposits credited to players?

Deposits are credited to the player's account in your cashier instantly once the payment is confirmed.

How and when do I get settled?

Settlement is T+0 — same day — in USDT, according to the agreed settlement and conversion terms in your commercial agreement.

What volumes can you handle?

Daily processing capacity of ₹1 crore. VIP players get a ₹5 lakh single-transaction limit and a ₹20 lakh daily limit per player.
